# Hoju Compass growth and revenue roadmap

Updated: 24 August 2026

This is an internal operating plan. It does not authorise a new tracking service, mailing list, advertising account, affiliate agreement or paid provider ranking. Those changes require a privacy, disclosure and commercial review before launch.

## Positioning

Hoju Compass should be the place a Korean newcomer opens when an Australian term, form, bill or decision is unfamiliar. The durable advantage is not the number of links. It is the combination of:

- a clear Korean explanation
- a current official source
- a next action or free tool
- a reason to return and continue

Google recommends people-first content that leaves the reader able to complete their goal. Publishing high volumes of shallow summaries purely for search traffic should be avoided. Each new guide should answer one real decision completely enough that the reader does not need another generic article.

## Content production system

Every priority topic should produce a small content package:

1. One detailed site guide with official sources, update date and a related tool.
2. One 5–7 card Korean carousel summarising the decision, not the entire article.
3. One 20–40 second vertical video built around a single mistake or checklist.
4. Two short community posts that answer a real question and link only when the full guide adds value.
5. One follow-up reminder or checklist inside My Compass when the topic has a future date.

Do not copy the same caption to every platform. Instagram and TikTok should lead with the mistake or visual comparison; Naver Blog can carry a longer Korean search summary; Facebook groups and Kakao communities should receive a direct answer first and a link second.

## Next editorial clusters

Build connected clusters rather than isolated posts. The next twelve high-intent topics are:

### Renting and moving

- Share house agreement types: co-tenant, sub-tenant and rooming accommodation
- Urgent repairs and how to keep a written repair record by state
- End-of-lease notice, final inspection and bond refund evidence
- Electricity bill terms: actual read, estimate, NMI and tariff

### Work and money

- Public holiday work: how to check the applicable Award and penalty rate
- First tax return after a Working Holiday or Student visa
- What to do when a Super contribution is missing from the fund account
- Refund, repair or replacement: change of mind versus consumer guarantee

### Everyday safety

- Australian beach flags, rip currents and when not to enter the water
- Heat, UV Index and bushfire or severe-weather alerts by state
- Parcel delivery, fake toll messages and address-change scams
- First-aid and emergency English phrases with TIS and Healthdirect pathways

Each cluster should link to an existing calculator or checklist and end with the next related guide. Update the oldest high-traffic guide before adding a fifth article to the same cluster.

## Organic acquisition

### Search

- Public searches for the exact brand, the domain plus Korean resume intent, and the `/resume-job-ad-checker` URL returned no Hoju Compass listing.
- Production `/`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml` and `/resume-job-ad-checker` returned HTTP 200. Robots allows all crawlers and names the canonical sitemap, so no public crawl block was found.
- Name-only Vercel environment inspection found none of `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION`, `BING_SITE_VERIFICATION` or `NAVER_SITE_VERIFICATION`. No value was opened and no search-console submission was made.
- Keep copy and price fixed while this discovery prerequisite is unresolved. After an authenticated owner creates each webmaster property, add only the supplied public verification token to the matching Production variable, redeploy, confirm verification and submit `https://hojucompass.com/sitemap.xml` once. Search-console mutation remains an owner-approved external action.

- Keep one page for one clear search intent. Avoid several pages competing for the same phrase.
- Use natural Korean titles that include the Australian term people see on the form or bill.
- Preserve Article and Breadcrumb structured data, sitemap dates, RSS and canonical URLs.
- Request indexing in Google Search Console after a new cluster is complete, not after every minor wording edit.
- Review impressions with low click-through rate for title or description problems; review clicks with poor engagement for content mismatch.
- Add a unique, relevant 1200 px or wider landscape image to priority articles before targeting Google Discover. Google describes Discover traffic as supplemental and recommends relevant high-resolution images rather than generic logos or text-heavy graphics.

### Social and community distribution

- Publish three recurring series: “호주 첫 주”, “돈 새는 순간”, and “계약 전에 3분”. Repetition builds recognition better than unrelated daily posts.
- Use the same visual system: navy, warm gold, one key number and one action per card.
- Turn comments into the next article only when the question repeats and can be answered from reliable sources.
- Partner first with Korean university societies, language schools, migration community organisations and working-holiday creators through useful co-created checklists, not paid praise.
- Never scrape group member emails or send unsolicited messages. Community rules and platform posting limits must be followed.

### Return visits

- Link every guide to My Compass, a checklist, calculator or reminder when there is a real next action.
- Use PWA install and page sharing after the reader completes a task, not as an entry pop-up.
- Introduce an optional newsletter only after consent, sender identification, unsubscribe, retention and deletion processes are ready.
- A useful newsletter cadence is fortnightly: one changed rule, one practical checklist and one upcoming deadline. Do not send generic traffic roundups.

## Paid acquisition experiments

Paid ads should promote a free, high-intent outcome rather than the home page or a display-ad page.

Recommended first landing pages:

- Salary Calculator for Korean workers already employed in Australia
- Property Inspection Checklist during peak rental periods
- Resume Builder before university graduation and seasonal hiring
- Tax Return Guide from May through October

For each experiment:

1. Use one audience, one promise and one landing page.
2. Add a unique UTM campaign with the existing Campaign Link Builder.
3. Set a fixed small loss limit before launch.
4. Measure completed tool use or return visit, not only link clicks.
5. Stop placements that produce short visits without tool completion.
6. Do not buy low-quality traffic, paid clicks or email lists; AdSense prohibits artificial impressions and certain incentivised or unsolicited traffic sources.

External ad account setup and budget spending require owner approval immediately before launch.

## Revenue ladder

### Stage 1 — premium outcomes

Keep one-off products aligned with a stressful, finite task:

- Resume Pro
- Rental Application Pack
- Pay Evidence Pack
- EOFY Pack
- Leaving Australia Pack

The free tool should solve the basic problem. Pro should save repeated work, produce an export or organise evidence; it should not hide essential safety or legal information.

### Stage 2 — disclosed affiliate referrals

Potential categories include SIM plans, travel insurance, bank or transfer services, tax agents and moving services. Add a category only when:

- the service can be compared using published criteria
- the commercial relationship is clearly disclosed before the link
- at least one useful non-affiliate option remains
- the recommendation can be removed quickly if complaints or terms change
- claims and eligibility are checked by the operator

Affiliate commission must never change an editorial ranking without an obvious commercial label.

### Stage 3 — sponsored guides and directory listings

Create a paid listing product for licensed local providers only after the provider policy is public. A listing can include contact details, service area, languages, licences supplied by the provider and a sponsored label.

Do not sell “Top tier”, “verified” or guaranteed-quality status merely because a provider paid. Organic rankings need a published scoring method, conflict disclosure, review moderation, complaint process and right of reply. Safety-critical trades also need a jurisdiction-specific licence and insurance checking procedure.

### Stage 4 — qualified lead marketplace

A quote-request marketplace can earn a fixed lead fee or completed-booking fee from plumbers, electricians, movers, cleaners, tax agents and other providers. It requires more than a contact form:

- clear identity of the contracting provider
- consent for the request and its data to be sent to named providers
- collection of only the information needed for the quote
- deletion and access procedures
- provider onboarding, licence and insurance checks where applicable
- complaint, refund and dispute process
- disclosure of how providers are selected and whether payment affects visibility
- secure storage and incident response

This is the highest-potential model but also the highest legal and operational risk. It should follow, not precede, audience trust.

### Stage 5 — selective display advertising

Display ads are a later supplement, not the main product. Test them only after traffic is large enough that a small number of placements can produce meaningful revenue.

Suggested operating thresholds, used as business guidelines rather than guarantees:

- Under 10,000 monthly pageviews: prioritise content, Pro conversion and direct partnerships; no display ads.
- 10,000–50,000 monthly pageviews: trial one direct sponsor or carefully disclosed affiliate category.
- Above 50,000 monthly pageviews: test one in-article or end-of-article display slot and compare revenue against page speed, completion and return visits.

Never place ads beside navigation, download, calculator result or primary action buttons where they can cause accidental clicks. Google permits only clear labels such as “Advertisements” or “Sponsored links” and prohibits asking visitors to click ads.

Before AdSense or another network is added:

- update the privacy policy for cookies, identifiers, IP addresses and third-party processing
- decide whether non-personalised advertising is the safer default
- implement any required consent and preference controls for the audiences served
- publish an advertising and affiliate disclosure
- reserve fixed-size ad spaces to prevent layout shift
- exclude checkout, account recovery, support, form and sensitive financial or health pages where ads would reduce trust
- verify mobile spacing and accidental-click risk
- monitor Core Web Vitals, policy notices and invalid traffic

## Email and personal information gate

Do not collect emails until the operational workflow is ready. Commercial email in Australia generally requires consent, accurate sender identification and a functioning unsubscribe mechanism. If a newsletter is launched:

- use an unchecked consent box with a clear description of frequency and content
- keep evidence of consent and the version of the notice
- send from the Hoju Compass domain
- include a one-click unsubscribe in every marketing email
- suppress unsubscribed addresses immediately
- define retention and deletion periods
- never add purchase-support contacts to marketing without the required expectation or consent
- do not buy, rent or scrape address lists

## Measurement dashboard

Review these once a month, by landing page and acquisition source:

- Search impressions, clicks and click-through rate
- Organic landing-page completion rate
- Tool completion or checklist progress
- Seven-day and 28-day return rate
- PWA installs and page shares
- Free-to-Pro checkout start and completed purchase
- Refund, support and access-failure rate
- Revenue per 1,000 sessions by source
- Content update age and broken official links

Do not publish customer-level or operator-only performance records on the public site. Aggregate reporting should avoid exposing personal data.
